# Issue #8: Plan for community fundraising

## Original Issue

Organisation: Food Bank Aid
Problem: The trusteess and staff of the charity are trying to improve our fundraising strategy. We want to pursue deeper relationships with community groups (sports clubs, local organisations, garden centres etc). We need a strategy for the next 6 months to execute on this which includes leading indicators / KPIs that we can measure to determine whether we are doing a good job. We have a large volunteer base (300+ volunteers) and a small staff (CEO, marcomms, fundraiser). We want to create a simple strategy for how to approach this problem over the next 6 months and give us the indicators that we need to make a decision on whether this can help us with our annual monetary shortfall (c. £500,000 a year).
